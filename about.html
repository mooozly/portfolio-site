About Me

A little bit of background and what I've been up to.

I started out studying engineering at the German University in Cairo, but it didn't take long to realize what I actually wanted was physics — so in 2024 I made the jump to RWTH Aachen, no regrets since. Lately I've been especially drawn to superconductivity and quantum technology (the idea of electrons moving with zero resistance still amazes me more than it probably should). Outside of physics, I tutor math and physics, and I'm slowly learning to windsurf and play bass guitar — with about equal amounts of grace in both.
